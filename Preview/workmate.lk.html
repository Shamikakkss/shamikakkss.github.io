Core Platform Modules

Powerful tools engineered for seamless client-to-worker dispatching & transparent job tracking

SOS / Emergency Job Dispatch

Tag critical tasks like electrical short circuits or major pipe leaks as EMERGENCY to broadcast high-priority alerts to all nearby active workers.

GPS Navigation & Map Pinning

Clients drop precise location pins on interactive maps while workers view real-time distance and turn-by-turn navigation routes.

Live Status Tracking

Real-time progress stepper updating both Client & Worker: Job Posted ➔ Worker Assigned ➔ On The Way ➔ Work Started ➔ Completed.

Live In-App Chat & Direct Call

Direct real-time messaging, click-to-call direct dialer, and instant message launching for smooth coordination once a job is assigned.

Clerk Multi-Role Engine

Secure identity management supporting CLIENT, WORKER, EMPLOYER, and ADMIN roles with custom onboarding data sync.

Neon Cloud Serverless PostgreSQL

High-performance serverless relational database managed via Prisma 6 ORM with SSL encryption and connection pooling.

System Process Workflow

Seamless 5-step workflow for clients & workers

1
Job Post & GPS Pin
Client posts task requirements with GPS coordinates
2
Worker Accepts
Nearby skilled worker accepts job on interactive map
3
On The Way
Worker drives to location with turn-by-turn navigation
4
Work Started
Technician performs repair & maintenance service
5
Complete & Review
Job completion marked and 1-5 star review submitted
