Ongoing Project

Workmate.lk — Location-Based Skilled Worker Marketplace

PickMe / Uber for Handymen & Technicians in Sri Lanka

Executive Summary

Workmate.lk is a state-of-the-art, location-based, on-demand service marketplace designed specifically for Sri Lanka. It connects clients who need urgent or routine repair, maintenance, and technical services with skilled local professionals (Electricians, Plumbers, Mechanics, AC Technicians, Carpenters, Painters, etc.) in real time — operating with the speed and convenience of PickMe or Uber for Skilled Workers.

Key Features & Modules

🚨 SOS Emergency Dispatch
Highlight critical issues (e.g. Electrical Short Circuit, Burst Pipe) to dispatch instant high-priority alerts to nearby workers.
📍 GPS & Leaflet Live Mapping
Interactive Leaflet maps showing exact GPS coordinates, real-time distance calculation (e.g. 1.8 km away), and turn-by-turn routes.
📊 PickMe-Style Live Status Stepper
Real-time status updates: Job Posted ➔ Worker Assigned ➔ On The Way ➔ Work Started ➔ Completed & Reviewed.
💬 Live Communication Triggers
In-app direct messaging, click-to-call (tel:), and automated WhatsApp chat initiation between Client and Worker.
👤 Worker Radius & Online Toggle
Workers can toggle Online/Available status and set customized preferred work radiuses (e.g., 15.0 km).
🔐 Clerk Multi-Role Auth
Multi-tenant role-based access control protecting Client, Worker, Employer, and Admin portals.
